A LETTER TO VICKY — PHOTOGRAPH PLACEMENT GUIDE
==============================================

Place your real photographs of Vicky inside this `images/` folder using the
exact filenames below. The website will automatically display them in their
frames without needing to edit any code.

Required / Recommended Photograph Filenames:

1. vicky-hero.jpg
   Where it appears: Hero section ("To My Beautiful Daughter, Vicky.")
   Recommended aspect: Vertical / portrait orientation (e.g., 3:4 or 4:5)

2. vicky-portrait-1.jpg
   Where it appears: "Her Story" section ("A Beautiful Story Still Unfolding.")
   Recommended aspect: Vertical / portrait orientation (3:4)

3. vicky-portrait-2.jpg
   Where it appears: "Memories" editorial collage (second featured portrait)
   Recommended aspect: Vertical or square orientation

4. vicky-memory-1.jpg
   Where it appears: "Memories" collage — Large primary frame
   Recommended aspect: Landscape or square (4:3 or 1:1)

5. vicky-memory-2.jpg
   Where it appears: "Memories" collage — Tall frame
   Recommended aspect: Portrait (3:4)

6. vicky-memory-3.jpg
   Where it appears: "Memories" collage — Wide frame
   Recommended aspect: Landscape (16:9 or 3:2)

7. vicky-memory-4.jpg
   Where it appears: "Memories" collage — Closing frame
   Recommended aspect: Landscape or square (4:3)

Included Aviation & Sky Photographs (already present, optional to replace):
- aviation-sky.jpg     (Her Journey backdrop)
- aviation-wing.jpg    (Her Journey wing above clouds)
- aviation-horizon.jpg (Final Message closing horizon)

Tip: Standard .jpg or .jpeg files work immediately. If you prefer .png or .webp,
you can either save them as .jpg or update the filename in index.html.
