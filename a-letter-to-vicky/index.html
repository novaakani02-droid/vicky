02 · A Letter From Dad

Words Kept for Always

V Personal Correspondence · From Dad to Vic

My Dear Daughter,

If there is one thing I hope you never forget, it is that you are loved, deeply and unconditionally.

Life has a beautiful way of bringing people into our lives and giving us reasons to be grateful. I may not have been there from the very beginning of your story, but I am grateful beyond words that I have had the privilege of being part of it. Somewhere along the way, you became more than someone I was responsible for. You became my daughter, and that is a place you will always hold in my heart.

Today, as you pursue your dreams and work towards becoming the pilot you aspire to be, I want you to know just how proud I am of you.

The journey may not always be easy. There will be long days, difficult moments and challenges that test your strength. But I hope you never lose sight of the beautiful future you are working towards.

Even when the distance between us feels great, remember that you are never alone. My thoughts, prayers and love go with you wherever life takes you.

As Scripture reminds us in 1 Thessalonians 5:18, we should give thanks in all circumstances. Today, I am thankful for you, for the bond we share and for the beautiful person you continue to become.

Keep reaching for the skies, my daughter. May God guide your steps, protect your journey and grant you the grace to fulfil every beautiful dream in your heart.

No matter how high you fly or how far life takes you, never forget that you will always have a home in my heart.

With all my love,

Dad.
